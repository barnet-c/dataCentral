About Us

Most local businesses aren't losing to competitors. They're losing to leaks.

The Problem

Behind almost every local business (a contractor, a clinic, a law firm, a real estate office) is an owner who is genuinely great at the work, and quietly overwhelmed by everything around it. Calls go unanswered during the busiest hour of the day. Quotes are sent once, then forgotten. Customers stop coming back, and no one notices until the numbers do. And the owner, who should be running the business, spends evenings on data entry instead of with their family.

None of this is dramatic. That's exactly what makes it dangerous. It doesn't show up as one lost sale or one angry customer. It shows up gradually, as a slow, invisible tax on revenue that most businesses have simply learned to accept as the cost of doing business.

What We Believe

We built DataCentral around a simple observation: these four leaks (response, follow-up, retention, and admin) are close to universal, and every one of them can be closed with the same kind of AI-driven system already running quietly in the background at larger, better-resourced companies.

What's been out of reach for local businesses was never the idea. It's been the cost, the complexity, and the in-house expertise it's taken to build it. We exist to close that gap.

How We Work

We don't start with a giant transformation project. We start with one leak, usually the fastest one to fix and the easiest one to see, like an instant text-back the moment a call is missed, because an owner should see a real result in the first week, not the first year.

From there, we build out the rest of the system leak by leak, always mapped back to the Four Leaks framework, so every project has a clear reason to exist and a clear number attached to it. Our goal isn't to sell software. It's to give owners back the revenue that's already theirs, and the time they started the business for in the first place.

Stop the leaks. Keep what's already yours.
