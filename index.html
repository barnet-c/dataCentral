Every local business leaks in the same four places.

No matter the industry, revenue drains through the same four gaps. Most owners have never mapped them. We have — and we've built a system for closing each one.

Leak 01

Response

The leads you never even know you lost.

What it is

Inbound calls, forms, and messages that go unanswered or answered too slowly — especially after hours and at peak times.

What it costs

Revenue that never enters the pipeline. You never even know it happened.

Leak 02

Follow-up

Money you already earned, never collected.

What it is

Quotes, proposals, and consultations that get one touch and then go cold.

What it costs

The largest recoverable pool in most businesses — revenue already earned but never collected.

Leak 03

Retention

The customers who just stop coming back.

What it is

Customers who quietly stop coming back. No reactivation, no reviews, no referrals.

What it costs

Slow erosion of the base, plus weaker local reputation and lead flow.

Leak 04

Admin

Your time, buried in busywork.

What it is

Owner and staff hours consumed by repetitive internal work — email, data entry, documents, reporting.

What it costs

The owner's time, which is the scarcest resource in the business.
