We organize everything we build around the Four Leaks, so you always know exactly what problem a service solves, and what it's worth to fix it.

01
Leak 1

Response

Every call, form, and message is a chance to earn a customer, or lose one to whoever answers first. These are the systems that make sure it's always you.

AI Voice Agent

Effort: High Impact: Highest

Answers calls 24/7, handles FAQs, books and reschedules appointments, sends confirmations, transfers to a human when needed.

How it's delivered

A voice platform wired into your calendar and CRM, so a booking taken at 2am is on the schedule by morning.

AI Receptionist (overflow)

Effort: Medium Impact: High

Picks up only when all lines are busy or out of hours, so nothing goes to voicemail.

How it's delivered

The same voice stack, scoped to the overflow. Your team still answers every call it can reach.

Missed-Enquiry Text-Back

Effort: Very Low Impact: High

Any unanswered call triggers an SMS within 30 seconds; replies route to a shared inbox.

How it's delivered

Sits on top of the number you already publish. Nothing about how you take calls changes.

Web Chat & Social Support

Effort: Medium Impact: Medium

Answers on the website, SMS, WhatsApp, and social DMs from one knowledge base.

How it's delivered

One knowledge base behind every channel, so the answer is identical wherever it is asked.

Speed-to-Lead Router

Effort: Very Low Impact: High

Form submission triggers a response within 60 seconds and assigns an owner.

How it's delivered

Connects your existing forms to your inbox and CRM. Speed is most of the win.

After-Hours Triage

Effort: Medium Impact: High

Distinguishes genuine emergencies from everything else; escalates or books accordingly.

How it's delivered

A voice agent with escalation rules you write, so a real emergency still reaches a real person.

Call Scoring & Coaching

Effort: Low Impact: High

Transcribes and scores every call against a booking rubric; weekly digest flags calls that should have converted.

How it's delivered

Scored against a rubric you approve, so coaching points are specific rather than a feeling about the week.

Where to start: missed-enquiry text-back. It takes hours rather than weeks, touches nothing else in your stack, and by the end of the first week you can count the replies it recovered.

02
Leak 2

Follow-up

Most sales aren't lost to a competitor. They're lost to silence. These systems make sure every quote, proposal, and lead gets followed up until it's won or closed.

Quote & Proposal Follow-up

Effort: Low Impact: Highest

Anything unapproved after 48 hours enters a sequence, then escalates to a human task.

How it's delivered

Watches your CRM or job system for quotes that have gone quiet, and stops the moment one replies.

Dormant-Opportunity Mining

Effort: Medium Impact: Highest

Pulls declined or expired quotes, filters by age and relevance, and generates targeted outreach.

How it's delivered

Reads the quotes already sitting in your system. Usually the largest recoverable pool in the business.

AI Sales Agent

Effort: High Impact: High

Qualifies inbound leads, answers questions, books meetings, updates the CRM.

How it's delivered

A chat or voice agent that writes back to your CRM, so the record is current without anyone typing it up.

Proposal & Quote Generator

Effort: Medium Impact: High

Turns notes, voice memos, and photos into a branded good / better / best proposal.

How it's delivered

Your notes in, your template out. Nothing reaches the customer before you have read it.

Pipeline Hygiene Agent

Effort: Low Impact: Medium

Flags stale deals, missing fields, and unassigned leads; nudges the owner weekly.

How it's delivered

Reads your CRM on a schedule and sends one short list of what needs a decision.

Outbound & Lead Generation

Effort: Medium Impact: Medium

Prospect discovery, list building, and personalized email or LinkedIn sequences.

How it's delivered

Sent from your own domain against a target list you sign off on, never a bought one.

03
Leak 3

Retention

It's far cheaper to keep a customer than to find a new one. These systems catch quiet churn before it happens, and turn happy customers into your best source of new business.

Review & Reputation Engine

Effort: Very Low Impact: High

Requests a review after every completed job; routes unhappy responses privately to the owner first.

How it's delivered

Triggered by job completion in the system you already use. Compounds, because local ranking drives lead flow.

Customer Reactivation

Effort: Low Impact: High

Identifies customers past their normal return interval and runs a win-back campaign.

How it's delivered

Segments your customer list by how long a return usually takes, then writes the offer that fits each group.

Renewal & Retention

Effort: Low Impact: High

Tracks expiring plans, contracts, and subscriptions; automates renewal nudges and failed-payment recovery.

How it's delivered

Reads renewal dates and failed payments straight from your billing system, so nothing lapses silently.

Referral Engine

Effort: Very Low Impact: Medium

Asks for a referral at the point of maximum satisfaction and tracks the result.

How it's delivered

One message, sent at the right moment, tracked through to the job it produced.

Post-Service Follow-up

Effort: Very Low Impact: Medium

Day-3 check-in and satisfaction capture, feeding both the review engine and service recovery.

How it's delivered

A single scheduled message after every job, with replies landing in an inbox a person reads.

04
Leak 4

Admin

The busywork that eats an owner's week rarely shows up on a P&L, but it's the most expensive leak of all, because it's your time. These systems take the repetitive work off your plate.

Owner's Weekly Scorecard

Effort: Low Impact: High

One email every Monday morning: volume, conversion, revenue, missed enquiries, reviews, receivables.

How it's delivered

Pulls from your CRM, accounting, and review profiles into one page you can read with a coffee.

AI Knowledge Assistant

Effort: Medium Impact: High

Trained on your SOPs, manuals, pricing, and policies, so staff and customers can find answers without asking you.

How it's delivered

Built from documents you already have, and every answer cites the page it came from.

AI Email Assistant

Effort: Low Impact: High

Categorizes the inbox, drafts replies, and surfaces what actually needs the owner.

How it's delivered

Connects to the mailbox you already use. Drafts wait for you — nothing sends on its own.

Document Processing

Effort: Medium Impact: High

Extracts and validates data from invoices, intake forms, contracts, and applications.

How it's delivered

Reads the document, checks it against your rules, and anything it is unsure of goes to a person.

Process Automation

Effort: Medium Impact: High

Automates CRM updates, invoicing, approvals, onboarding, and handoffs between systems.

How it's delivered

Runs across the tools you already pay for, in the order your team already works.

Recruiting Responder

Effort: Very Low Impact: High

Replies to applicants within five minutes, screens, and offers self-scheduled interviews.

How it's delivered

Triggered by your job ads, with interview slots taken from your real calendar.

Internal Help Desk

Effort: Low Impact: Medium

Answers repeat HR and IT questions for staff.

How it's delivered

The same knowledge assistant, pointed at internal documents and available to staff only.

Analytics Assistant

Effort: High Impact: Medium

Answers plain-language questions about your own operating data.

How it's delivered

Reads your existing exports or reporting database, and shows the figures behind every answer.
